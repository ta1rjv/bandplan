About this site #

For every band the page gives the BTK legal allocation, the IARU Region 1 band plan, licence class access and channel plans, together with source and confidence labels. The legal allocation (BTK) and the recommended usage (IARU Region 1) are shown as separate layers; see IARU vs TR comparison. This site is informational; the binding text is the BTK regulation (Regulation).

Licence classes #

  • Class A: all bands allocated to the amateur service in the table, subject to the table conditions.
  • Class B: the sub-ranges named for class B in the table (for example 7000-7100 kHz on 40 m and 28000-29700 kHz on 10 m; own-built equipment, at most 5 W).
  • Class C: only 144-146 MHz and 430-440 MHz; at most 5 W.
