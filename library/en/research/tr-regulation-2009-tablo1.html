Tablo-2 footnotes (summary, English) #

  1. Amateur may get primary status here once fixed service is accommodated elsewhere.
  2. Spot frequencies 3.5, 7.0, 10.1, 14.0, 21.0, 24.89, 144 MHz used by authorities in emergencies; amateurs may assist in cooperation.
  3. Band also usable for amateur-satellite.
  4. Band primary for fixed service in TR; 10100 kHz spot usable in emergencies in cooperation with authorities.
  5. Morse and digital modes only.
  6. F3E and G3E not used in 28.000-29.500 MHz.
  7. Handheld FM max output 5 W.
  8. EME permitted.
  9. International amateur DX use.
  10. Operation per IARU R1 and national plan, per BTK band plan annexed to national frequency plan.
  11. J3E only in 1832-1835 kHz.
  12. Must accept interference from ISM and SRD devices.
  13. Club repeaters max output 25 W.
  14. Class B: homebrew equipment max 5 W, 7.000-7.100 MHz and 28.000-29.700 MHz (education/encouragement).
  15. 50-52 MHz usable with BTK permission.
  16. Class C: 144-146 and 430-440 MHz, max output 5 W.

EK-4 rules on classes (paraphrase) #

  • A: all Tablo-1 frequencies permitted for class A, within Tablo-1 criteria.
  • B and C: at club / school / emergency / scouting / research stations or at a class A station, under the supervision of the responsible operator, may use ALL Tablo-1 bands and emissions; otherwise only frequencies permitted for their class.
