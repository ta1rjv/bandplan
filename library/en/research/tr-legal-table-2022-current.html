MADDE 22 key paragraphs (summary) #

  • (9) Class A: all Table frequencies permitted for class A, within table criteria.
  • (10) Class B and C: at club/school/emergency/scouting/research stations or a class A station, under the responsible operator's supervision: ALL bands and emissions; otherwise only frequencies permitted for their class.
  • (11) Unlicensed third parties may take part for training, with responsible operator present.
  • (12) Repeaters by clubs; experimental and internet-radio interface (e.g. EchoLink) activity allowed, following BTK frequency planning.
  • (13) Encryption forbidden; listed open methods (Q codes, Morse, RTTY, SSTV, FSTV, PSK, PACTOR, AMPR, APRS, TCP/IP) allowed.
  • (3)(4) Emergency/disaster support; with permission, use of authorities' frequencies.

Resolved conflict #

Unofficial "BTK Milli Amator Telsiz Band Plani Ozeti (2022)" (Wikimedia, TA1HYK) giving 100 W/400 W on 160/80/40 m MATCHES the 2022 criteria. The earlier conclusion that it was an error was based on the 2018 version and is WRONG.
