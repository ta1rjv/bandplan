MADDE 21 - operative text (quoted, Turkish + English) #

Source: [local source file, not published] lines 1879-1916.

"MADDE 21- (1) Amator telsiz istasyonlari; Tablo-25'te belirtilen teknik olcutlere uygun olmak kaydiyla amator telsizciler tarafindan kullanilir." EN: "Amateur radio stations are used by amateur radio operators, provided they comply with the technical criteria specified in Table-25."

"(2) Esaslari KEGM tarafindan belirlenen amator telsizcilik belgesine sahip gercek kisiler ile sorumlu operatorun mesuliyetinde olmak kaydiyla amator telsiz dernekleri, egitim ve ogretim kurumlari, acil durum ve afet haberlesmesinde gorev alan kuruluslar, izcilik kuruluslari ve arastirma kurumlari amator telsiz istasyonu kurabilirler." EN: "Natural persons holding an amateur radio license (principles set by KEGM), as well as amateur radio associations, education/training institutions, emergency/disaster-communication organizations, scouting organizations, and research institutions may establish amateur radio stations, under the responsibility of a responsible operator."

"(9) A sinifi amator telsizcilik belgesi sahipleri; tum amator telsiz istasyonlarinda bu sinifa izin verilen ve Tablo-25'te belirtilen frekanslari yine ayni tabloda belirtilen teknik kriterlere uymak kaydiyla kullanirlar." EN: "Holders of a Class A license may use, at any amateur station, the frequencies permitted for this class as specified in Table-25, subject to the technical criteria in the same table."

"(10) B ve C sinifi amator telsizcilik belgesi sahipleri; amator telsiz dernekleri, egitim ve ogretim kurumlari, acil durum ve afet haberlesmesinde gorev alan kuruluslar, izcilik kuruluslari ve arastirma kurumlarinda kurulu istasyonlarda veya bir A sinifi amator telsizciye ait istasyonda, istasyonun sorumlu operatoru gozetiminde ve sorumlulugunda, Tablo-25'te belirtilen butun amator frekans bandlarini ve emisyon tiplerini kullanabilirler. Istasyondan sorumlu amator telsizcinin gozetimi disinda veya kendi adlarina kayitli istasyonlarda ise ancak belge siniflarinin musaade ettigi frekanslarda faaliyetlerde bulunabilirler." EN: "Class B and C license holders may use ALL amateur frequency bands and emission types in Table-25, at stations of clubs/schools/emergency orgs/ scouting orgs/research institutions, or at a Class A operator's station, UNDER SUPERVISION of the station's responsible operator. Outside such supervision, or at their own registered stations, they may only operate on the frequencies their license class permits."

"(11) A sinifi belge sahibi amator telsizcilere ait istasyonlarda ... bir sorumlu operatorun hazir bulunmasi halinde; belge sahibi olmayan ucuncu sahislarin, egitim ve ozendirilmesi amaciyla, amator telsiz haberlesmesine katilmalarina izin verilir." EN: "At Class A operators' stations (and club/school/etc. stations), with a responsible operator present, unlicensed third parties may participate in amateur communication for training/encouragement purposes."

"(12) Amator telsizciler, amator telsiz dernekleri tarafindan kurulan tekrarlayici (role) sistemleri araciligi ile amator faaliyetlerde bulunabilirler." EN: "Amateur operators may operate via repeater systems established by amateur radio associations."

Footnote quotes from Tablo-25 (verbatim, ASCII):

  • 40 m (7000-7100 kHz): "Egitim ve ozendirme amaci ile B sinifi amator telsizcilik belgesi sahibi olan telsizciler, kendi imalatlari olan ve en fazla 5 W cikis gucundeki bir cihaz ile HF bandinda 7000-7100 kHz frekans bandinda haberlesme yapabilecektir." (lines 1951-1956) EN: "For training/encouragement, Class B license holders may communicate in 7000-7100 kHz using homebrew equipment of their own construction, max 5 W output."
  • 10 m: identical wording applied to "28000-29700 MHz" [sic, source literally prints MHz - near-certain typo for kHz] (lines 2034-2039).
  • 2 m: "El telsizlerinin frekans modulasyon modunda azami 5 W verici cikis gucunu gecemeyecektir." EN: "Handheld radios shall not exceed 5 W TX output in FM mode." (2053-2055)
  • 2 m: "Bu bandda amator telsiz dernekleri tarafindan kurulacak olan role cihazlarinin azami cikis gucu 25 W seviyesini gecemeyecektir." EN: "Club repeaters in this band shall not exceed 25 W output." (2058-2061)
  • 2 m: "C Sinifi amator telsizciler tarafindan 144-146 MHz frekans bandinda verici cikis gucu 5 W seviyesini gecmeyecek sekilde haberlesme yapilabilecektir." EN: "Class C operators may communicate in 144-146 MHz with TX output not exceeding 5 W." (2062-2065)
  • 70 cm: same rule text, applied to "430-440 MHz frekans bandinda" (2077-2080).

2. Per-class access resolved (A / B / C) #

VERIFIED from MADDE 21 paragraphs 9-11 (quoted above) plus Tablo-25 explicit class columns:

  • Class A: full privileges - all bands, all power/PEP limits in Tablo-25, at own registered station (para. 9).
  • Class B/C AT A SUPERVISED STATION (club/school/emergency/scouting/research org, or a Class A operator's station, under that station's responsible operator): may use ALL bands/emissions in Tablo-25 (para. 10) - supervised B/C is NOT limited to the B/C-marked rows.
  • Class B/C AT THEIR OWN STATION, UNSUPERVISED: limited to frequencies their class is explicitly permitted (para. 10, 2nd sentence). Explicit class-restricted rows in Tablo-25:
  • 6 m (50-52 MHz): A and B only. No Class C access found.
  • 2 m (144-146 MHz): A, B, C; Class C capped 5 W TX.
  • 70 cm block: Class C capped 5 W TX (430-440 MHz); explicit per-row class letters not visible in the extracted text for this block - INFERRED to match 144-146 MHz treatment (A/B/C) based on identical restriction wording pattern and TRAC Adana's parallel layout; UNVERIFIED at PDF-cell level.
  • 23 cm (1240-1300 MHz): A and B.
  • 5650 MHz-142 GHz block: grouped under the same "A and B" marker printed once at the 23 cm row in both btk_ftm_olcutler.txt and TRAC Adana - INFERRED for the following rows, not independently restated per row.
  • HF bands below 50 MHz: Class A only, EXCEPT the two explicit Class-B homebrew (max 5 W) carve-outs at 7000-7100 kHz and 28000-29700 kHz. No Class C access below 50 MHz found anywhere in Tablo-25.
  • Class B homebrew rule: max 5 W, home-built equipment only, restricted to 7000-7100 kHz and 28000-29700 kHz (10 m footnote literally says "MHz" in the source - flagged as a probable typo for kHz, not to be copied to the site without a corrective note).
  • Class C general limit: 5 W max output, confined to 144-146 MHz and 430-440 MHz - i.e., Class C = VHF/UHF handheld-class only per Tablo-25; no HF or microwave access identified for Class C.
  • Repeater power: club-association repeaters on 144-146 MHz max 25 W output. No explicit W cap found for 70 cm repeaters in Tablo-25 (only that 431.550-431.825 MHz and 439.150-439.425 MHz are reserved for association-built repeaters, without a stated number) - UNVERIFIED whether the 25 W cap also applies to 70 cm; neither TAD v1.0 nor TRAC Adana state a number either.
  • Handheld limits: 5 W max TX FM on 144-146 MHz, 432 MHz block, and 1240-1300 MHz (repeated footnote).

KEGM exam regulation's class definitions (Sec. 5) still literally cite the 2009 "Ozel Telsiz Sistemleri Yonetmeligi EK-4 Tablo-1" rather than Tablo-25 - see Conflicts/Unresolved.

160 m / 80 m / 40 m power discrepancy - specific analysis #

Tablo-25, TAD v1.0 (BTK-official, dated one month after the Teknik Olcutler took effect), and the TRAC Adana mirror ALL THREE independently agree: 160 m = 9 W/30 W PEP; 80/40 m = 75 W/75 W. No evidence was found (mevzuat.gov.tr, resmigazete.gov.tr, btk.gov.tr searches) of a Board decision or amendment 2018-2026 raising these to 100 W/400 W. The Wikimedia (2022) document is a self-described compilation by a named individual (TA1HYK), not an official BTK publication, carries no document/version reference beyond "(2022)", and its 160/80/40 m figures do not match any of the three agreeing sources. Most plausible explanation: compiler transcription error (e.g. mixing the 20-m-and-above PEP column into the 160/80/40 m rows), not a genuine regulatory change.

Conclusion: 9 W/30 W (160 m) and 75 W/75 W (80 m, 40 m) are the CURRENT LEGAL values. Wikimedia's 100 W/400 W figures should be marked INCORRECT / excluded as a site source unless a primary citation is later found.

5. KEGM exam regulation - class definitions and privileges #

Source: [local source file, not published] - "KIYI EMNIYETI GENEL MUDURLUGU AMATOR TELSIZCILIK SINAV VE BELGELENDIRME YONETMELIGI" (Coastal Safety General Directorate Amateur Radio Examination and Certification Regulation).

"MADDE 18 - (1) A sinifi belgesi: Ozel Telsiz Sistemleri Yonetmeliginin ekinde yer alan EK-4 Amator Telsiz Istasyonlari'nin Tablo-1'inde belirtilen tum frekans bantlarinda, belirlenen guc sinirlarinda, izin verilen emisyonlari kullanabilme olanagi saglar. (2) B sinifi belgesi: ... EK-4 Amator Telsiz Istasyonlari'nin Tablo-1'inde B sinifi icin belirtilen frekans bantlarin, belirlenen guc sinirlarinda, izin verilen emisyonlari kullanabilme olanagi saglar. (3) C sinifi belgesi: ... EK-4 Amator Telsiz Istasyonlari'nin Tablo-1'inde B sinifi icin [sic - literal repeat of "B sinifi" in source, likely a drafting typo for "C sinifi"] belirlenen Spektrumun bir bolumune dusuk guc seviyelerinde kisitli erisimi saglar."

EN: "MADDE 18 - (1) Class A license: provides the ability to use all frequency bands in Table-1 of Annex-4 (Amateur Radio Stations) of the Special Radio Systems Regulation [2009 instrument], within specified power limits, permitted emissions. (2) Class B: provides ability to use bands specified for Class B in Table-1 of Annex-4. (3) Class C: provides restricted, low-power access to a portion of the spectrum specified [literally repeats 'Class B' in the source] in Table-1 of Annex-4."

CONFLICT FLAGGED: This KEGM regulation, as extracted, still cites the 2009 "Ozel Telsiz Sistemleri Yonetmeligi EK-4 Tablo-1" by name - the instrument reported repealed by RG 30608 (2018-11-27). No amendment marker updates this reference to the FTM Teknik Olcutler/Tablo-25 in the local extract. Either (a) a genuine un-amended stale cross-reference in KEGM's own regulation (a real legal-drafting gap between two agencies' instruments), or (b) this research pass has an outdated copy of the KEGM regulation. NOT VERIFIED which. Practical effect on class DEFINITIONS is likely immaterial (2009 Table-1 and Tablo-25 assign classes A/B/C to essentially the same band set per Section 3), but this cross-reference inconsistency should be reported to the user, not silently resolved.

Exam structure (MADDE 14, 16):

"MADDE 14 - (1)(a) A ve B sinifi: Butun ilgili amator radyo duzenlemeleri, haberlesme teknolojileri isletim prosedurlerini ve Ozel Telsiz Sistemleri Yonetmeliginde belirtilen amator telsizcilerin kullanacagi frekans bandlari ve teknik kriterleri kapsar. (b) C Sinifi: Pratik ve temel haberlesme teknikleri ve ilgili ulusal ve uluslararasi isletme duzenlemeleri ve ekipman kullanimi ile birlikte Ozel Telsiz Sistemleri Yonetmeliginde belirtilen bu sinif amator telsizcilerin kullanacagi frekans bandlari ve teknik kriterleri kapsar."

EN: exam syllabus for A/B covers "all relevant amateur radio regulations, communication-technology operating procedures, and the frequency bands and technical criteria ... specified in the Special Radio Systems Regulation" [same stale 2009 reference]; C syllabus covers "practical and basic communication techniques and relevant national/international operating regulations and equipment use, together with the frequency bands and technical criteria for this class specified in the Special Radio Systems Regulation."
