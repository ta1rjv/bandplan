Licence Class System

Band access of class A, B and C amateur licences and the ARTICLE 22(10) supervised operation exception.

In Turkey, amateur radio licences are issued in three grades, class A, B and C; the examination and certification process is carried out by KEGM (Directorate General of Coastal Safety). [1] The frequency/power/emission access conditions are defined per class in the BTK 2022 Technical Criteria table (ARTICLE 22). [2]

Class A #

Class A licence holders may operate in all bands allocated to the amateur service in the table, within the conditions of the table (output power, emission type, restrictions within the band). [2]

Class B #

Class B licence holders may operate in the sub-ranges specified separately for class B in the table. For example, in the 40 metre band (7000-7100 kHz) and the 10 metre band (28000-29700 kHz), for training and encouragement purposes, they may communicate only with home-built equipment of at most 5 W output power. [2] The class B access conditions outside these two bands are listed in the TR legal status table on the relevant band pages.

Class C #

Independent access for class C licence holders is limited in the current (2022) table to the 2 metre (144-146 MHz) and 70 centimetre (430-440 MHz) bands only; the maximum output power is 5 W in both. [2]

Supervised operation exception (ARTICLE 22/10) #

Class B and C operators, while operating under the responsible operator's supervision at a club, school, or research station, or at a class A station, may use ALL table frequencies and emission types. Outside that context, they may only use the frequencies permitted for their own class.

[2]
