IARU vs TR Comparison

The two data layers on the band pages (BTK legal allocation and IARU Region 1 recommendation), how to read the chart and what the badges mean.

Two different sources, two different meanings #

The charts and tables on every band page show two independent data layers on top of each other (overlay); they do not merge them:

  • Turkey (BTK 2022): legal allocation status - BTK Technical Criteria decision no. 2022/IK-SYD/245, ARTICLE 22. [1] This layer shows what is legally permitted in Turkey.
  • IARU Region 1: voluntary usage recommendation - the IARU Region 1 HF Band Plan (as of 16 October 2020) [2] and, for 50 MHz and above, the IARU Region 1 VHF Handbook, version 10.03. [3] This layer is a mode/usage recommendation; it is not part of TR legislation. Where the Handbook and the older one-page summary plans conflict at 50 MHz and above, the Handbook is taken as authoritative. [3]

Differences between the two layers (e.g. a band that is not allocated in Turkey but appears in the IARU plan, or a use that TR permits but the IARU plan does not recommend) are deliberately not hidden; they are shown on separate rows in the chart.

Reading the chart #

In the spectrum chart on each band page, the upper row shows the Turkey legal status (green: allocated, hatched pattern: not allocated; in the lower sub-row, class A/B/C access bars), and the lower row shows the IARU Region 1 usage segments (coloured by mode). Triangle markers show specific (spot) frequencies. Because the chart is only a visual summary, tables containing the same data are placed below the chart (for keyboard and screen reader access).

Colour coding alone does not carry meaning: every segment also has a text label/tooltip, and the palette has been chosen to be suitable for colour-blind users.

Status and confidence badges #

Allocated in TR / Not allocated in TR - shows whether a band is allocated to the amateur service in Turkey's 2022 table.

Verified / Secondary source / Unverified - shows how directly/clearly the value is printed in the source document:

  • Verified: the value is printed directly and clearly in the source document.
  • Secondary source: the value was taken from a reliable secondary source (e.g. a news/report summary) rather than from the primary source itself.
  • Unverified: the source cell is merged, illegible or missing; the value has not been guessed and is explicitly marked "unverified".

Frequency units and whole-number Hz representation #

In the data layer of the site every frequency is stored as an integer in Hz (e.g. 144.000 MHz = 144000000 Hz); this avoids rounding/binary-fraction errors in kHz/MHz/GHz displays. On screen the value is converted to the unit appropriate to the band group (kHz for LF/MF, MHz for VHF/UHF, GHz for SHF/EHF) and shown with an explicit unit label; the unit is never changed silently.
