Three-symbol designator scheme (general rule) #

The explanation below is a general radio notation rule derived from ITU-R Appendix 1; it is not specific to the BTK table and is not attributed to a particular source.

Symbol Meaning Example values occurring on this site
1st symbol (letter) Type of modulation of the main carrier A: double-sideband AM, F: FM, G: phase modulation, H: full-carrier SSB, J: suppressed-carrier SSB, R: reduced/variable-level carrier SSB
2nd symbol (digit) Nature of the modulating signal 1: single channel of digital information without a subcarrier (e.g. keyed CW), 2: single channel of digital information with a subcarrier, 3: single channel of analogue information (e.g. voice)
3rd symbol (letter) Type of information transmitted A: telegraphy for aural reception (Morse/CW), B: telegraphy for automatic reception, C: facsimile, D: data/telemetry, E: telephony (voice), F: television/video

Example: A1A = double-sideband AM carrier (A), digital without subcarrier (1), telegraphy for aural reception (A) - that is, keyed Morse/CW. J3E = suppressed-carrier SSB (J), single-channel analogue (3), telephony/voice (E).

Emission designators printed in the TR table #

The codes below are taken directly from the source for the bands whose emission cell is printed completely and legibly in the current (2022) BTK table (2200 m, 160 m, 80 m, 10 m). [1]

2200 m, 160 m, 80 m, 10 m (full list printed): A1A, A1B, A1D, A2A, A2B, A3F, A3J, F1A, F1D, F2A, F2B, F3E, F3F, G3E, H3E, J2A, J2B, J2C, J2D, J3C, J3E, J3F, R3E

For the full list per band, see the "Turkey legal status" table on the relevant band page.

Research gap: merged/illegible cells #

In the source table, the emission cells of the following bands are printed merged or in a way that cannot be separated visually: 630 m, 60 m, 6 m, 2 m, 70 cm and 23 cm and all bands above. The emission list for these bands has not been guessed; on the relevant band pages it appears as "merged cell - unverified" (a value that is not printed in the source is never guessed). [1]
