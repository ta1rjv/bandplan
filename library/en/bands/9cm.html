What can be done on this band? #

Notes specific to Turkey #

The 3400-3475 MHz (9 centimetre) band is NOT allocated to the amateur radio service in the current BTK 2022 amateur table. Therefore amateurs in Turkey cannot legally transmit in this band. The information below is given for general context only, based on the IARU Region 1 band plan.

Propagation character (context - IARU Region 1) #

This microwave range is limited entirely to line-of-sight; rain scatter and Earth-Moon-Earth (EME) techniques can be used. Atmospheric absorption begins to become more pronounced at these frequencies.

Typical modes and activities (context - IARU Region 1) #

In the IARU Region 1 plan the lower part of the band contains an all-mode segment including an EME and local beacon centre of activity, followed by a section reserved for beacon use only, a segment for satellite downlinks, and a wide unlabelled all-mode section.

Spot frequencies #

No separate centre of activity (spot) frequency is defined for this band.
