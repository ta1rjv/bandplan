What can be done on this band? #

Propagation character #

The 76 GHz (4 millimetre) band is in the millimetre-wave range; atmospheric absorption is very high at these frequencies and practical range is generally limited to line-of-sight and short distances. Operation on such bands requires advanced experimental equipment and careful link budget calculation.

Typical modes and activities #

The lower part of the band is a preferred segment for the amateur-satellite service; the middle and upper parts consist of unlabelled all-mode use and a second amateur-satellite segment - this second segment contains a narrowband centre of activity preferred in non-CEPT countries. The very top of the band is for non-preferred (secondary priority) all-mode use.

Spot frequencies #

No separate centre of activity (spot) frequency is defined for this band.

Notes specific to Turkey #

The band is open to class A and B amateurs in Turkey, but the TR allocation does not cover the whole 75.500-81.500 GHz range of IARU Region 1: only 75.5-76 GHz is allocated. The band is also used for the amateur-satellite service. In the TR source table the output power data for this band is marked as low-confidence (not clearly printed in the source).
