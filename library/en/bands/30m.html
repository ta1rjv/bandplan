What can be done on this band? #

Propagation character #

30 metres behaves as a transition band between 40 and 20 metres; because it is usable in both day and night and has relatively light traffic, it is preferred especially by CW and narrowband digital mode operators. Being a WARC band, it is not used for contests, which keeps it quieter and well suited to DX work.

Typical modes and activities #

The band is limited to CW and narrowband digital modes; voice (phone) modes are not part of the IARU Region 1 plan for this band. The lower segment is reserved for CW and the upper segment for narrowband digital modes.

Spot frequencies #

10116 kHz is the centre of activity for stations working CW QRP (low power).

Notes specific to Turkey #

The band is allocated to class A amateurs in Turkey. According to the TR table, the fixed service has primary status in this band; therefore only CW and digital modes may be used, a restriction that already matches the general character of the IARU Region 1 plan.
