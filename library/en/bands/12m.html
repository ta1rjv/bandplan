What can be done on this band? #

Propagation character #

12 metres is a WARC band between 15 and 10 metres and its propagation depends strongly on solar activity; during high-sunspot periods daytime intercontinental DX openings occur, while during low-activity periods the band is mostly closed. Short-to-medium distance openings caused by Sporadic-E are also possible.

Typical modes and activities #

The lower part of the band is reserved for CW and the middle part for narrowband digital modes. The range 24929-24931 kHz is reserved exclusively for International Beacon Project transmitters. The remainder of the band is open to all modes including SSB.

Spot frequencies #

24906 kHz is the CW QRP centre of activity. 24950 kHz is the SSB QRP centre of activity and 24960 kHz is the digital voice centre of activity.

Notes specific to Turkey #

The band is allocated to class A amateurs in Turkey; the TR table states that this band may move to primary status in the future and that it is also used for the amateur-satellite service.
