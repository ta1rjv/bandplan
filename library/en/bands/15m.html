What can be done on this band? #

Propagation character #

15 metres is a band highly sensitive to solar activity: when sunspot numbers are high it offers excellent daytime conditions for long-distance DX contacts, while during low solar activity the band can stay closed for most of the day. Unexpected short-to-medium distance openings can also occur during Sporadic-E (sporadic ionization of the E layer) periods.

Typical modes and activities #

The lower part of the band is reserved for CW and the middle part for narrowband digital modes; in the range 21110-21120 kHz all modes except SSB can be used. The range 21149-21151 kHz is reserved exclusively for International Beacon Project transmitters. The upper part of the band is open to all modes including SSB and also permits amateur-satellite use.

Spot frequencies #

21055 kHz (QRS) and 21060 kHz (QRP) are centres of activity (CW). 21180 kHz is the digital voice calling frequency, 21285 kHz is the SSB QRP centre of activity, and 21340 kHz is the centre of activity for image modes.

Notes specific to Turkey #

The band is allocated to class A amateurs in Turkey and is also used for the amateur-satellite service.
