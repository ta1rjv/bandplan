What can be done on this band? #

Propagation character #

6 metres is known as the "magic band"; most of the time it is limited to normal tropospheric range, but during periods when Sporadic-E layers form (especially in the summer months) sudden and strong openings reaching very long distances can occur. During periods of high solar cycle activity, longer-distance DX openings through F2-layer reflections may also be possible; in addition, communication by non-ionospheric paths, using meteor scatter and Earth-Moon-Earth (EME) techniques, is also practised on this band.

Typical modes and activities #

The lower part of the band is reserved for CW (including the Synchronized Beacon Project), the middle part for SSB/CW, and the upper parts for narrowband digital modes, EME and meteor scatter activity. The all-mode segments also contain FM simplex, digital voice internet gateways and FM/DV repeater channels; the upper end of the band is for all-mode use, divided into blocks according to national planning.

Spot frequencies #

50050 kHz is the future international centre of activity for CW and 50090 kHz is the CW intercontinental centre of activity. 50110 kHz is a centre of activity used only for the intercontinental calling, and 50150 kHz is the international centre of activity. 50305 kHz is the PSK centre of activity, 50630 kHz is the digital voice calling frequency, and 51510 kHz is the FM calling channel.

Notes specific to Turkey #

The band is open to class A and B amateurs in Turkey; the TR allocation is limited to 50-52 MHz (the IARU Region 1 plan extends up to 54 MHz, but the range 52-54 MHz has no legal counterpart in Turkey). Earth-Moon-Earth (EME) communication can also be carried out on this band; the maximum output power of beacons is 25 W.
