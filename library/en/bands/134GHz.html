What can be done on this band? #

Propagation character #

The 134 GHz (2 millimetre) band is close to the upper limits of the millimetre-wave range; atmospheric absorption is very high and practical range is limited to line-of-sight and short distances. Amateur activity on these bands is largely experimental and requires advanced equipment.

Typical modes and activities #

The lower part of the band is reserved for the amateur-satellite service; it is followed by a small all-mode segment containing a narrowband centre of activity and an unlabelled all-mode section. The upper part of the band is for non-preferred (secondary priority) all-mode use.

Spot frequencies #

No separate centre of activity (spot) frequency is defined for this band.

Notes specific to Turkey #

The band is open to class A and B amateurs in Turkey and is also used for the amateur-satellite service. In the TR source table the upper limit of this band is printed as 142 GHz, whereas the IARU Region 1 table ends at 141 GHz. The difference between these two sources has been carried over here as it is, not corrected. In the TR source table the output power data for this band is marked as low-confidence (not clearly printed in the source).
