Licence class access #

  • Class A: Whole band (subject to the table conditions)
  • Class B*: 7000–7100 kHz, 5 W - Home-built equipment only, maximum 5 W.

* This class's access is limited to a sub-range narrower than the whole band; appearing in the coarse class list does not mean full-band access.

Class B and C operators, while operating under the responsible operator's supervision at a club, school, or research station, or at a class A station, may use ALL table frequencies and emission types. Outside that context, they may only use the frequencies permitted for their own class.

What can be done on this band? #

Propagation character #

40 metres is a classic night/transition band, varying considerably across the day-night transition and suited to both local/regional and intercontinental contacts. During the day regional contacts generally dominate; at night, when the D layer disappears, range increases significantly and intercontinental DX conditions can develop. Strong broadcast stations in the 41 metre shortwave broadcast band directly above this band can cause interference at frequencies near the upper edge of the band, especially in the evening and at night.

Typical modes and activities #

The lower part of the band is reserved for CW, the middle part for narrowband digital modes (including common digital modes such as FT8/FT4), and the upper part for all modes including SSB. The upper part of the band is preferred for SSB contests; the topmost segment is considered the priority for intercontinental work.

Spot frequencies #

7030 kHz is the centre of activity for CW QRP (low power) and 7090 kHz for SSB QRP. 7070 kHz is the digital voice calling frequency, and 7165 kHz is the centre of activity for image modes.

Notes specific to Turkey #

The band is open to class A and B amateurs in Turkey. For training and encouragement purposes, class B licence holders may operate in the lower part of the band (in the range 7000-7100 kHz) only with home-built equipment of at most 5 W output power; there is no such sub-range or power limit for class A.
