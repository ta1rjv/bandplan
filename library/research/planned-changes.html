Turkiye-HAMBandPlan project. Scope: WRC-23 outcomes, WRC-27 outlook, IARU Region 1 activity 2023-2026, and Turkey (BTK). Emergency-communication topics excluded. All dates today = 2026-09-25.

1. WRC-23 Outcomes #

1.1 Agenda Item 9.1 topic b) - 1240-1300 MHz (23 cm) vs RNSS #

Status: VERIFIED Source: [local source file, not published] (IARU Summary Meeting Report, WRC-23, by Barry Lewis G4SJH), page 3 (lines 102-113), confirmed again on page 5 (lines 149-179, "Attachment 1 - Relevant extracts from WRC-23 Document 338 - approved in the WRC-23 7th plenary meeting, 8th December 2023").

Verbatim adopted footnote text (new RR footnote number 5.A91B - note: "A91B" is the WRC-23 provisional/working numbering used in Document 338; the definitive permanent RR footnote number as published in the RR 2024 edition was not located in the supplied text extracts and remains UNVERIFIED):

"5.A91B Administrations authorizing operation of the amateur and amateur-satellite services in the frequency band 1 240-1 300 MHz, or portions thereof, shall ensure that the amateur and amateur-satellite services do not cause harmful interference to radionavigation-satellite service (space-to-Earth) receivers in accordance with No. 5.29 (see the most recent version of Recommendation ITU-R M.2164). The authorizing administration, upon receipt of a report of harmful interference caused by a station of the amateur or amateur-satellite services, shall take all necessary steps to rapidly eliminate such interference. (WRC-23)"

Table-of-allocations change (same source, page 5, lines 156-166):

"MOD B15/338/5 ... 1 240-1 300 ... Amateur ... 5.282 5.330 5.331 5.332 5.335 5.335A ADD 5.A91B"

What this requires of administrations/amateurs (source, page 2, lines 88-94):

"the meeting eventually moved away from the incorporation by reference approach and coalesced on a well-supported compromise for footnote text to be added in Article 5 of the RR. The footnote refers to the amateur and amateur-satellite entries in the frequency allocation table. It reminds administrations and amateurs of the need to protect the primary RNSS from interference and points to the new Recommendation ITU-R M.2164."

Net effect, explicitly stated (page 3, lines 115-121):

"In effect the new footnote provides: regulatory certainty for the protection of RNSS receivers from harmful interference, guidance to administrations if interference to RNSS receivers from amateur transmissions occurs, continued amateur access to the 1240-1300 MHz frequency band."

Table allocations themselves were not changed - Recommendation ITU-R M.2164 was not incorporated by reference (page 2, lines 82-86: CEPT's proposal to incorporate M.2164 by reference was not adopted; other regions favored NOC - No Overall Change).

Entry into force (source, page 4, lines 140-142):

"The outcome from WRC-23 will eventually appear in the 'WRC-23 Final Acts' for inclusion into the next edition of the RR around mid-2024. The new regulations are likely to enter into force from 1 January 2025."

1.2 Resolution 774 (WRC-19) suppressed #

Status: VERIFIED Source: same file, page 3 line 112 ("Additionally Resolution 774 (WRC-19) is suppressed which brings the activity to a conclusion") and page 5 lines 177-183 ("SUP B15/338/13 RESOLUTION 774 (WRC-19) Studies on technical and operational measures to be applied in the frequency band 1 240-1 300 MHz to ensure the protection of the radionavigation-satellite service (space-to-Earth)"). Original Resolution 774 text (background, for context only) is in [local source file, not published], confirming it was the resolution that mandated the 4-year ITU-R study leading to Reports ITU-R M.2513, M.2532 and Recommendation ITU-R M.2164.

Status: VERIFIED Source: iaru_r1_wrc23_23cm_report_2023-12.txt, page 4, lines 134-138:

"However consequential activities may continue especially in the CEPT countries where a regional work item is open to develop a spectrum decision on this same topic but focussed on the Galileo RNSS system. The IARU Region 1 will continue to be active in that work." This is an open/ongoing regional (CEPT) process, not a completed WRC-23 outcome - should be labeled PLANNED/ONGOING on the site, not IMPLEMENTED.

1.4 ITU-R Report M.2532 (technical basis) #

Status: VERIFIED (existence and scope only) Source: [local source file, not published]. Confirms the report's title/scope: "Amateur and amateur-satellite services characteristics and usage" in the 1240-1300 MHz band, produced in response to Resolution 774 (WRC-19) "resolves to invite the ITU Radiocommunication Sector ... to perform a detailed review of the different systems and applications used in the amateur service and amateur-satellite service allocations in the frequency band 1 240-1 300 MHz" (lines 110-114). No amateur-specific conclusion/recommendation text beyond this scope statement was extracted in this pass; treat any numeric claims (power levels, antenna gains, duty cycles) from M.2532 as requiring a dedicated follow-up read of that file if needed for the site.

1.5 Other WRC-23 items (10 GHz IMT Region 2, space weather) #

Status: SECONDARY (ARRL news article, not a local text extract; not re-verified this cycle - carried over from prior research pass) - 10.0-10.5 GHz: a footnote limited to about a dozen Region 2 countries for IMT/broadband, rather than a region-wide designation (ARRL, https://www.arrl.org/news/wrc-23-concludes-with-wins-for-amateur-radio-and-agenda-items-designated-for-the-next-two-events). - Space weather sensors: "a clear definition for such sensors was confirmed, with frequency protection being agreed upon as an agenda item for WRC-27" (same source). Frequency protection itself is deferred to WRC-27, not decided at WRC-23.

2. WRC-27 #

2.1 Dates and location #

Status: VERIFIED Source: https://www.itu.int/wrc-27/ (direct fetch): "18 October - 12 November 2027", "Shanghai, China". Note: the IARU AC Oct 2025 summary record (iaru_ac_2025-10_summary.txt, page 7, line 271-273) as of October 2025 still described this as "dates to be determined" - the exact dates were finalized by ITU after that AC meeting:

"7.1 WRC-27 - Vice President Wrede reported on preparations for the upcoming Working Party 5A meeting in November. The ITU has announced that Shanghai, China will be the location for WRC27 on dates to be determined."

2.2 Governing resolution #

Status: SECONDARY (search-engine synthesis of itu.int document metadata for document S24-CL-C-0064, not a direct text read; PDF full text not extractable by available tools) - Resolution 813 (WRC-23) is the resolution that sets the actual WRC-27 agenda (adopted at WRC-23, Dubai, Dec 2023), superseding the earlier preliminary agenda in Resolution 812 (WRC-19). - itu.int document abstract (S24-CL-C-0064): "Resolution 813 (WRC-23) recommends agenda items" for WRC-27. - WRC-27 agenda "includes 19 specific agenda items plus standing items" (search-engine summary, not independently verified from primary text).

2.3 Amateur-relevant WRC-27 agenda items #

Status: UNVERIFIED / not independently confirmed from primary text this cycle. Two PDF sources that would contain the exact item numbers/text (RSGB's ITU-WRC-27-Agenda.pdf and ITU's Resolution 813 (WRC-23) PDF at https://www.itu.int/dms_pub/itu-r/oth/0c/0a/R0C0A0000110038PDFE.pdf) could not be rendered as text by the available WebFetch tool (compressed PDF streams). Only the following, previously reported as SECONDARY via search-engine summaries of iaru.org content, could be carried over - do not publish specific agenda item numbers on the site without a direct primary-source read of one of the two PDFs above: - 1300-1350 MHz band-protection item adjacent to 23 cm. - A space-weather-related item, with amateur HF bands (0.1-20 MHz) and 28/50 MHz reportedly removed from the item's scope. - A lunar/Moon-communications item reportedly modified to exclude 430-440 MHz. - An item on broadband/IMT expansion touching 3.3 GHz and 10 GHz (Region 2).

Direct primary confirmation obtained this cycle for one adjacent matter (not a WRC-27 agenda item per se, but active AC business relevant to 430-440 MHz): Source: iaru_ac_2025-10_summary.txt, page 3, lines 103-122 (IARU AC Vice President Wrede's report, Oct 2025):

"Mr. Wrede addressed the recent threat to the 70 cm band from AST & Science LLC (operating as AST SpaceMobile) to deploy commercial satellites for direct-to-handheld voice communications in Low Earth Orbit that utilize spectrum allocations within the 430-440 MHz amateur and amateur satellite bands for Telemetry, Tracking, and Command (TT&C) operations... on August 29th, 2025, the FCC Space Bureau did grant a license to AST & Science LLC for using the 430-440 MHz amateur radio allocation for TT&C operations but for only 20 additional satellites (instead of 243) and only for Launch and Early Orbit Phase (LEOP) and emergency operations during a period of maximal 24 hours... The IARU maintains its view that the use of Article 4.4 of the ITU Radio Regulations is inappropriate in this matter." This corroborates the "UHF Spectrum Situation" input paper (RSGB, VI26_C5_11) identified for the Vienna 2026 conference in the prior research pass - same underlying issue (AST SpaceMobile use of 430-440 MHz).

2.4 IARU position status (as of Oct 2025) #

Status: VERIFIED Source: iaru_ac_2025-10_summary.txt, page 7, line 274:

"The IARU WRC27 position paper has been prepared and will be distributed to member societies." Source: page 2, lines 77-81 (President Ellam, general WRC-27 posture, applies to all agenda items including amateur-relevant ones): "He challenged the AC to keep this in mind as we discuss our position on the various Agenda Items that our role should continue to be 'defensive' rather than seeking new allocations. We simply should not waste our limited resources (both financial and people) on chasing additional frequencies given the present environment." Source: page 5, lines 190-195 (Region 3 participation in preparatory groups): "Mr. Widjaja and Region 3 EC member Jack Hantongkom HS1FVL attended the 2nd meeting of APG27 held in July/August 2025, in Pattaya, Thailand. It was noted that, while it is still in the preliminary stage, they presented IARU preliminary positions on WRC-27 agenda items concerned to IARU. Next APG27-3 meeting will be held in Japan in July 2026." No CEPT/ECC-specific WRC-27 position text was located in the supplied local files (CEPT's ECC CPG WRC-27 agenda page, https://www.cept.org/ecc/groups/ecc/cpg/page/agenda-for-wrc-27, was identified but not fetched - remains a "document to download" if needed).

3. IARU Region 1 #

3.1 26th General Conference, Zlatibor, Serbia, 1-4 November 2023 #

Status: SECONDARY / partially VERIFIED Direct primary confirmation obtained only for one Zlatibor decision, via a later reference in the EC minutes: a Youth Coordinator nomination recommendation. Source: iaru_r1_ec_minutes_2025-05-21.txt, page 6, lines 238-241:

"The recommendation from the Zlatibor General Conference request the member societies to nominate Youth Coordinators. Recommendation ZL23_C3_Rec_3 (Document ZL23_C3_50) Societies without appointed youth coordinator should appoint a dedicated Youth Coordinator within next year." Also referenced: ongoing IARU-R1 constitution work "as decided during the Zlatibor conference" (same file, page 7, lines 335-336). No HF or VHF/UHF/SHF band-plan decision text from Zlatibor 2023 was found in any supplied local file or successfully fetched primary source. This remains an evidence gap - do not state on the site that Zlatibor 2023 changed the HF or VHF band plans; state only that HF/VHF/SHF items were on its agenda (per the prior research cycle's search-summary evidence, still unconfirmed by primary text).

3.2 Interim Meeting, Paris, France, 24-25 April 2025 #

Status: VERIFIED Source: iaru_r1_ec_minutes_2025-05-21.txt (EC+ meeting, Zoom, 21 May 2025 - follow-up to the Paris Interim Meeting), page 1. Held alongside "REF 100-year celebration" (IARU centenary) and Region 1's 75th anniversary (page 1, lines 27-30; confirmed again in iaru_ac_2025-10_summary.txt page 4, line 139: "the IARU Centennial celebration held in Paris in April was a huge success").

Recommendations relevant to this site (HF/VHF committees; EMCOMM/EMC and pure-administrative items excluded per scope):

C4 (HF Committee): - PA25_C4_03 "HF Harmonisation and Digital Voice" (RSGB, G6JYB) - approved by EC. Source page 2, lines 48-54:

"It is recommended that digital voice activity should be in the all mode segments of the HF bands, which would typically coincide with the current all modes segments for other voice modes such as SSB, AM and FM... The recommendation was approved by the EC" - PA25_C4_04 "HF Harmonisation and Emergency Centres of Activity (CoA)" (RSGB, G6JYB) - approved by EC. Source page 2, lines 57-63 (note: despite the title mentioning "Emergency," the substance is about HF band-plan flexibility, not emergency-comms procedure, so included here): "Whilst the Emergency Centres of Activity are capable of adjustment to accommodate future HF band plan harmonisation, it is important to preserve their flexibility and not limit them to any one specific mode in band plans. The recommendation was approved by the EC" - PA25_C4_07 "Establish a working group to propose update of the CEPT HAREC Radio Amateur License syllabus" (NRRL, LA6VQ) - working group to be established, deadline explicitly stated. Source page 2-3, lines 66-90: "The working group should complete its work by 15 January 2026." EC decision (page 3, lines 117-136): EC supports the review but "postpone[s] the final decision on this recommendation until the EC meeting following HAM RADIO 2025"; syllabus content is HAREC licensing (education), not a band plan, but flagged as an active/open item with a hard 15 Jan 2026 deadline.

C5 (VHF/UHF/SHF Committee) - directly relevant to the site's 23cm/1.3GHz content: - PA25_C5_05 "Review of the 23cm band plan" (UBA, ON6TI). Source page 4, lines 143-155:

"Recommendation: Establishment of a working group for the reallocation of the 23cm band." EC decision (amended per G4SJH, accepted by EC): "The amended recommendation now reads: Recommendation: Establishment of a working group for the reallocation of the 23cm band. To develop a proposal for the next General conference Invitation to be sent from the C5 chair to the MS VHF/UHF/Microwave Managers to participate in the working group Working Group lead G4SJH" No completion deadline was stated for this working group (unlike the HAREC one at 15 Jan 2026) - its deliverable target is explicitly "the next General conference" (i.e., the 27th General Conference, Vienna, Sept 2026 - see 3.4 below). - PA25_C5_10 "1.3GHz Background and Options" (RSGB, G6JYB) - approved (as a background/study recommendation, not a band-plan change). Source page 4, lines 157-179. Key criteria stated for future changes: "changes should be: Specific / Feasible to implement within a reasonable timeframe / Proportionate to amateurs / Reassuring with respect to regulatory concerns / Supportive of future amateur development" EC follow-up: "The EC requests the VHF+ committee to list what it is expected to study to follow the recommendation." - PA25_C5_12 "1.3GHz Background and Options" (RSGB, G6JYB) - approved by EC. Source page 5, lines 184-190: "Proactively ending FMATV in 1258-1300 MHz by December 2026 / Explore options on a national basis for coordinated DATV repeater outputs just above 1300MHz ... / Exploit this exercise to demonstrate goodwill to regulators in order to avoid disproportionate limitations on other amateur usage in 1.3GHz" This is a concrete, dated (December 2026), primary-sourced planned change to 1.3 GHz band usage - suitable for direct citation on the "planned changes" page. - PA25_C5_09 "Innovation & Opportunities above 275GHz" (RSGB, G6JYB) - approved by EC, encourages experimental access above 275 GHz and >100 GHz generally (page 5, lines 193-202). Out of scope for a Turkey-focused HF/VHF/UHF/microwave reference unless the site covers EHF, but included for completeness.

No C4/C5 HF or VHF core band-plan frequency-range changes (i.e., actual segment boundary edits) were adopted at this Interim Meeting - all C4/C5 items above are either harmonization guidance, background/study recommendations, or working-group establishments targeting the 2026 General Conference for final decisions. This distinction (recommendation vs. adopted band-plan change) must be preserved on the site.

No evidence of a newer officially published HF band plan version was found. Given PA25_C4_03/PA25_C4_04 above are still at "recommendation" stage (not yet incorporated into a published band plan) and Zlatibor 2023's HF-plan agenda outcome is unconfirmed (3.1), the correct site status label is "no confirmed HF band-plan update since 16 Oct 2020 as of this research date", not "confirmed unchanged."
