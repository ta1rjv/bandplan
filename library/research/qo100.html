NB transponder (VERIFIED, NB PDF p.3) #

Uplink 2400.000-2400.500 MHz, downlink 10489.500-10490.000 MHz. Downlink = uplink + 8089.500 MHz (derived from paired table edges).

Uplink MHz Downlink MHz Use
- 10489.500-10489.505 Lower beacon 10489.500, CW F1A 400 Hz shift, + guard
2400.005-2400.040 10489.505-10489.540 CW only
2400.040-2400.080 10489.540-10489.580 Digimodes, 500 Hz max BW
2400.080-2400.150 10489.580-10489.650 Digimodes, 2700 Hz max BW
2400.150-2400.245 10489.650-10489.745 SSB only, 2700 Hz max BW
- 10489.745-10489.755 Middle beacon 10489.750, 400 bit/s BPSK, + guards
2400.255-2400.350 10489.755-10489.850 SSB only, 2700 Hz max BW
2400.350-2400.495 10489.850-10489.995 Mixed modes (2700 Hz max BW) and special purpose
- 10489.995-10490.000 Experimental beacon 10490.000, CW and other modulations

SECONDARY: 10489.855 MHz broadcast spot (news services), RSGB 2022-10-14. Omit "emergency" wording (DEC-003).

WB (DATV) transponder (VERIFIED, WB PDF) #

Uplink 2401.0-2410.0 MHz RHCP; downlink 10490.5-10499.5 MHz horizontal. Beacon: uplink 2402.0, downlink 10491.5 MHz, DVB-S2 FEC 4/5, 1500 kS. Wide DATV (1 MS; 1.5 MS should use this part): downlink 10493.25, 10494.75, 10496.25 MHz (uplink 2403.75, 2405.25, 2406.75). Narrow DATV 333 kS/250 kS: 14 channels, downlink 10492.75-10499.25 MHz every 500 kHz; lower 9 may carry 500 kS. Very narrow 125/66/33 kS: 27 channels, 10492.75-10499.25 MHz every 250 kHz. Nets: 10499.25 MHz, 333 kS recommended.

Satellite facts #

  • Position 25.9 deg E (AMSAT-UK, SECONDARY). Launch 15 Nov 2018 (AMSAT-UK, SECONDARY).
  • Polarisation: uplinks RHCP; WB downlink horizontal (WB PDF, VERIFIED); NB downlink vertical (AMSAT-UK, SECONDARY).
  • Operational start date: UNVERIFIED (do not publish).

Turkey #

  • Legal (BTK 2022 table): 2400-2450 MHz NOT allocated to amateurs -> uplink not permitted. 10450-10452 MHz only in 3 cm -> QO-100 downlink range is outside the amateur allocation; RECEIVING (listening) does not require a transmit allocation.
  • TRAC document (p.1-2): "Ulkemizde yonetmelik geregi 10.5 GHz kullanima acik olmasina ragmen, 2.4 GHz maalesef TA Amatorlerine kapalidir." and "(Dikkat: 2.4GHz Turk Amator telsizcilere kapalidir)". Note: TRAC's "10.5 GHz open" wording refers to the band loosely; the table only lists 10450-10452 MHz.
  • Community activity (ta1d.com Sunday listen net on 10489.777 MHz): UNVERIFIED snippet - do not publish.
