Definitions (PAGE 3) #

  • All modes = CW, Phone, listed CoA modes, plus AM
  • Narrow band modes = up to 500 Hz BW (CW, RTTY, PSK, etc.)
  • Digimodes = any digital mode within BW (RTTY, PSK, MFSK, etc.)
  • Image modes = analogue/digital image (SSTV, FAX)

General notes/footnotes (PAGE 3) #

  • Frequencies are TRANSMITTED frequencies, not suppressed-carrier.
  • (1) Lowest LSB dial settings: 1843, 3603, 7053 kHz
  • (#) max BW unspecified, 500 Hz suggested
  • (##) Highest USB dial on 60 m: 5363 kHz
  • CW accepted across all bands except beacon segments (DV05_C4_Rec_13)
  • Sideband: below 10 MHz use LSB, above 10 MHz use USB, except 5 MHz band = USB
  • AM allowed in telephony sub-bands with consideration to adjacent users (NRRL Davos 05)
  • OUT OF BAND: max USB dial should be 3 kHz below upper band edge on 20 m-10 m
  • Contests not on 5, 10, 18, 24 MHz; non-contesters encouraged to use 30/17/12 m during major contests
  • Unmanned/automatic stations rules, emergency exercise bandwidth limit 2700 Hz (VA14_C4_Rec_06)

Remote operation rules (PAGE 4) #

Call sign = that of country where station is located; CEPT T/R 61-01 applies only to operators physically visiting; contest/award eligibility is up to organizers (SC11_C4_REC_07, VA14_C4_REC_04).

History section (PAGE 4) - key milestones #

  • 2005 Davos: introduced band-plan-by-bandwidth, eff. 1 Jan 2006
  • 2008 Cavtat: eff. 29 Mar 2009 - 40 m restructuring (CW 7000-7040; narrowband/digimode segments moved; Digital Voice CoAs introduced: 3630/7070/14130/18150/21180/24960/28330 kHz)
  • 2011 Sun City: eff. 17 Aug 2011 - CW contest-preferred 7000-7025 withdrawn; 29100-29200 kHz BW 2700->6000 Hz for FM simplex 10 kHz channels; FM repeater channels renumbered RH1(29520/29620)-RH8(29590/29690); FM Simplex Repeater 29610 introduced
  • 2014 Varna: eff. 26 Sep 2014 - 29000-29100 kHz BW 2700->6000 Hz; satellite segment 29300-29510: downlink restriction removed
  • 2017 Landshut: eff. 21 Sep 2017 - Digimode 500 Hz segment extended 10130->10150 kHz; new 200 Hz digimode segment 3570-3580 kHz
  • 2020 Novi Sad: eff. 16 Oct 2020 - removed 6 kHz BW restriction on 29000-29510 kHz (incl. satellite 29300-29510); designated 21125-21450 kHz for amateur satellites non-exclusive (frequencies >21400 kHz preferred) [see CONFLICT note above re: table vs. changelog]
